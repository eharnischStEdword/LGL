What it is

A read-only window into giving data that lives in Little Green Light (LGL). Fund-by-fund totals, time-range comparisons, year-over-year views, weekly collection tracking, and a copy-to-clipboard monthly Offertory snapshot for the bulletin. The dashboard never writes anything back to LGL.

The two views

New view (lgl.onrender.com/v2)

Answers first. The top row answers three questions: How was giving this week? Are we ahead of last year? Which funds moved? Below that: Recent Weeks (eight weekly bars), a chart with period and view controls, a computed Compare Years view, a ranked fund ledger, and a pivot table. The Copy for Bulletin button produces the same snapshot the Classic view does. This view will become the default once it has settled.

Classic view (lgl.onrender.com)

The original dashboard. Load Offertory Data (Offertory only) or Load All Funds Report (every fund), pick a time range (Fiscal Year, Year to Date, Last 12 or 24 Months, All, Year-over-Year, or a multi-year comparison), use the fund search and Fund Totals table to toggle funds on the chart, and use the Financial Snapshot copy button for the bulletin figure.

How the numbers arrive

  • Online gifts (Pushpay credit card) flow into LGL live and show up the same day.
  • Cash and checks are counted by the money counters on Wednesday, then entered, so they are usually in LGL by Thursday. That schedule is a plan, and it can slip.
  • Bulk imports are run by hand, normally Monday and Thursday. The top bar shows the date of the most recent import, and a "+N recent" note when live gifts arrived after it.

Because of this rhythm, the newest week shows as Counting (striped gold) until its cash and check count has actually been entered in LGL. The dashboard checks for the count itself; if the count runs late, the week simply stays in Counting. A Counting week is left out of averages and comparisons on purpose, so a half-counted Sunday never reads as a bad week.

Troubleshooting

  • Signed in but told you are not authorized: your email must be added to the allow-list; ask Eric to add you (ALLOWED_DASHBOARD_USERS in Render).
  • A new fund is missing: create it in LGL, then add it to the FULL GIVING REPORT scheduled export inside LGL, or it will never appear on the dashboard.
  • Numbers look a touch behind: data is a scheduled export plus a live top-up; a back-dated gift can lag until the next export. Known and accepted.
  • The newest week looks low: check its label first. If it says Counting, the cash and check count has not been entered yet; the number is online gifts so far.
  • Everyone got signed out: normal after a deploy or restart; sessions are in memory. Sign back in.
  • Year-over-Year labels look wrong in the Classic view: known deferred bug (it hardcodes 2025 and 2026, so labels drift after July 2026). The new view's Compare Years computes its years correctly.
  • All Funds view stops loading: LGL zips its export; the unzipper in server.js is the usual break point if LGL changes formats again.

If Eric is unavailable

  • Pushing to the main branch on GitHub auto-deploys. No database, no disk; worst case, redeploy and sign back in.
  • Secrets live in Render: the Microsoft client secret (CLIENT_SECRET; if it is removed, sign-in turns off entirely and the dashboard is open to anyone with the link) and the LGL API key (LGL_API_KEY, regenerated in LGL under Settings, then Integration Settings, then LGL API).
  • The export cadence lives in LGL itself: two scheduled report exports (Offertory, and FULL GIVING REPORT) configured under Gift/Pledge Reports, then Scheduled reports.
  • For anything involving Microsoft 365, Entra sign-in consent, or the parish tenant, the parish IT vendor is Elemental Technology Solutions; they administer the tenant.
  • Hosting (Render), the code (GitHub), and the Microsoft Entra app registrations are tied to Eric's accounts. The apps keep running without him, but deploys, secret rotations, and sign-in fixes need his access or a handoff of those accounts.
